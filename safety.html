Safety & Community Guidelines

Tonight — Play safe, be kind.

Tonight is for adults meeting up tonight. Most people are here for a good night out. These rules keep it that way.

Before you meet

  • Meet in public. A bar or busy public place, not someone's home or a car, for a first meet.
  • Tell a friend where you are going, who you are meeting, and when you expect to be back.
  • Keep your phone charged and your own way home sorted before you go.
  • Do not share your home address, workplace, or financial details in chat.
  • Trust your gut. If something feels off, do not meet — block and report instead.

While you are out

  • Consent matters, every time. "No strings" never means no consent. If someone says no, or changes their mind, that is the end of it.
  • Do not pressure anyone about drinks, drugs, sex, or going somewhere else.
  • Watch your own drink. Do not accept a drink you did not see poured, and do not leave it unattended.
  • If you feel unsafe, leave, tell staff, call a friend, or call emergency services (911 in Canada).

In the app

  • Be kind. No harassment, threats, slurs, or unwanted explicit messages or photos.
  • One photo of you, really you. No stolen photos, no impersonation, no fake profiles.
  • No paid sex, no solicitation, no illegal activity.
  • Report and block. If someone breaks these rules, report them in the app and block them. Reports go to support@tonightapp.ca. We review reports and may warn, suspend, or permanently remove accounts.
  • Under 18? Report it immediately. Tonight is 18+ only, no exceptions.

If something goes wrong

If you are harmed or threatened, contact emergency services first, then tell us at support@tonightapp.ca so we can act on the account. We will cooperate with law enforcement where required.
