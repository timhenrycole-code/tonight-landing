Tonight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Prepared November 2026. These pages are a starting point, not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address and date of birth (used for sign-in and the 18+ check).
  • Profile: one photo and a short line about your night, as you provide them.
  • Location: your device location while you are using Tonight, to show you people out tonight near you and calculate distance. Location is collected only while you are using the app — never in the background. Other users only ever see a rounded distance, never your exact location.
  • Messages: chat messages between matched users, so the chat feature works and so we can investigate reports.
  • Technical data: device type, app version, and basic usage/error logs, via our hosting and app providers (Emergent / Expo).

What we use it for

To run Tonight: show your profile to nearby users tonight, open chats on a match, keep the service secure, investigate reports and abuse, and fix bugs. We do not sell your personal information.

Who else sees it

  • Other users see your profile photo, your line about your night, and your approximate location while you are active tonight — that is the point of the app. They do not see your email or date of birth.
  • Our service providers (hosting, app platform) process data only to run the service.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Your "Available now" status switches off automatically after 24 hours. You can delete your account anytime in the app (Profile → Delete account): this permanently removes your profile, photos, matches, messages and availability, and cancels your subscription. Reports filed about an account may be kept for safety and legal reasons.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your profile in the app.
  • You can delete your account and personal information anytime in the app (Profile → Delete account), or by emailing support@tonightapp.ca — we will delete it unless the law requires us to keep something (for example, to investigate a report).
  • You can turn off location access in your device settings, but Tonight cannot show you people near you without it.

Security and limits

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no app is perfectly secure. Do not share passwords, home addresses, or financial details through Tonight.

Children

Tonight is 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a user is under 18, report them and contact us at support@tonightapp.ca.

Changes

We may update this policy; the version on this page is the current one. Contact: support@tonightapp.ca.
